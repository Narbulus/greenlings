A texture sheet for the game Valheim: 4 single leaves in a 2x2 grid on a plain flat pure white background, each leaf centred in its cell, pointing straight up, not touching the others. The four leaves differ: a long narrow willow-like leaf, a broad oval beech-like leaf, a pointed birch-like leaf with a serrated edge, and a three-lobed small oak-like leaf. Flat, front-on, no perspective.

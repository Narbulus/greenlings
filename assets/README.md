# Greenlings

Tame greylings (and greydwarfs, and brutes), earn their trust, and teach them to do your chores.

## Taming and trust

Drop **coins** near a wild greyling or greydwarf and stay out of sight. It nibbles them one at a time,
like a boar eats food, and before long it stops being hostile. Brutes won't stoop to coins: tempt
them with **amber** or **amber pearls**, and shamans with **thistle**.

A freshly tamed one is a **stray** with a name of its own (Grug, Skorby, Bjolf...). It wanders your
camp and gets up to mischief, but it won't learn anything yet. Earn its trust:
- **Feed it** berries, mushrooms, honey, carrots or cooked meat, from your hand or left on the ground.
- **Pat it** (E). It loves this, but a pat only counts every so often.
- **Spend time near it.**

After a treat or a pat a stray tags along behind you for a little while, which makes it easy to lead home.

As trust grows it turns greener and a little plant sprouts from the hollow of its head. At full trust it
becomes a **Greenling**. Every greenling has a trait or two (greedy, lazy, clumsy, curious, cuddly, magpie,
zoomy, copycat, sleepy); hover over it to see them.

## Getting around

**Pat** a greenling (E) to have it **follow you**; pat it again to make it **stay** (it goes back to its job).
Greenlings open ordinary doors on their own and close them behind them. Gates are too heavy.

## Teaching

1. **Give it a coin** (use one on it). It watches you closely.
2. **Show it a job**: take something from somewhere, then put it somewhere else. Keep showing it
   things and it keeps paying attention.
3. A little burst of sparkles means it understood. Off it goes, one item at a time.
4. For a minute afterwards it is still paying attention. Show it another step to make a two-step job
   (meat onto the cooking station, then cooked meat back into the chest), or show a step again to correct it.

Pat it while it is watching to say "never mind": it forgets its job.

Jobs stretch to fit: show it one torch and it keeps every torch nearby topped up, lowest first;
show it one beehive and it does them all, and picks up any honey lying around; a row of kilns is
worked as one; things go back into the chest that already holds them. Several greenlings watching
at once all learn the same job.

| Who | Can learn |
|---|---|
| **Greyling** | chest to chest; picking up loose items; foraging berries, mushrooms and flowers; collecting honey; keeping fires, hearths and torches fed; keeping a feeding spot stocked |
| **Greydwarf** | everything a greyling can, plus running machines (smelter, kiln, furnace, windmill, spinning wheel, cooking station, fermenter), gathering stone and flint, and breaking bushes for wood |
| **Shaman** | everything a greyling can, plus **planting**: plant a seed or two on tilled ground and it fills the patch in tidy (if slightly wonky) rows, and replants after you harvest |
| **Brute** | only smashing. Hit a log, rock or stump while it watches, and it smashes every one around and leaves the pieces for the little ones to carry |

### Learning by watching

A contented greenling sometimes wanders over just to see what you are up to. It picks up simple,
can't-go-wrong chores that way (putting things away, foraging, feeding fires) and may jump in to help.
Pick berries in front of it and it might run off and pick some for you.

### Tools

Planting needs a **cultivator** and repairing needs a **hammer**. Hand one over, or leave it in a chest
near the work and the greenling fetches it itself. You will see it in their hand.

### Shamans

Shamans look after the others: they heal hurt greenlings and your animals, and help strays trust you
faster by keeping them company and soothing them.

## Left to themselves

Greenlings with nothing to do find something useful now and then: tidying loose things into the
chest they belong in, giving a busy friend a hand, or doing a chore they picked up. Give one a
**hammer** and it goes round fixing worn pieces. When their job runs dry they wander off and play
rather than standing about.

They also play: tag, chasing your animals and the birds, and gathering in a ring for a natter.
Greenlings never raid your chests unless you taught them to.

## Upkeep

- **Food.** Hungry greenlings eat food left lying about. Leave some out, or teach one to keep a feeding
  spot stocked (take food from a chest, drop it on the ground); your animals will eat there too.
- **Nest.** Build a **Greenling Nest** (hammer, furniture tab: 6 wood, 3 resin). Each nest sleeps two.
  Greenlings sleep in them at night, pop back for a rest by day, and plant flowers around them.

Neglected greenlings never die or run away, but they work slower, get into more mischief and sometimes just sulk.

## Danger

Greenlings leave animals alone. When monsters turn up, greylings run to you (or to a brute) for
protection, or panic and scatter; pat a frightened one to calm it down. Greydwarfs throw rocks and
brutes fight. Wild greylings hate greenlings, so protecting them is your job. If one dies, it drops
whatever it was carrying.

# Teaching

## A lesson

1. **Use a coin** on a trusting greenling. It stops and watches you closely, following if you
   walk more than 6 m away.
2. **Show it a job**: take something from somewhere, then put it somewhere else, within 90 seconds.
3. A little burst of sparkles and a chime mean it understood. Off it goes, one item per trip.

Its attention lasts about 90 seconds. It drains three times as fast when you're far away, and every step you show
tops it back up. If it runs out, the greenling loses interest and goes back to what it was doing.

Pat it (++e++) while it's watching to say **never mind**: it forgets its job.

!!! tip "Several at once"
    Every greenling watching you learns the same lesson, so you can train a whole crew with one demonstration.

## Multi-step jobs

For a minute after a lesson the greenling is still **attentive**, and it doesn't need another coin:

- Show it **another step** to build a job of up to three steps. For example: raw meat from the chest onto the
  cooking station, then cooked meat from the station back into the chest.
- Show a step with the **same source or destination** again to **correct** it.

On each trip it does the first step that has both something to carry and somewhere to put it.

## Jobs stretch to fit

You only have to show something once, and the job covers everything of that kind nearby (within 20 m by default):

| You showed it | It does |
|---|---|
| one torch | every torch, brazier or hearth burning the same fuel, lowest first |
| one beehive | every beehive nearby, and it picks up any loose honey |
| one berry bush | every bush of that kind |
| one kiln in a row | the whole row (stations of one kind within 8 m are worked as a bank) |
| a chest as the destination | prefers whichever chest already holds that item and has room |
| a cooking station | collects whatever the station produces, including burnt food |
| a station that takes several items | brings any item the station accepts from the source chest |

## Learning by watching

A contented greenling sometimes wanders over just to see what you're up to, even without a coin.
It only picks up simple, **can't-go-wrong** chores that way:

- putting loose things, forage, honey or sap into a chest
- feeding fires
- (brutes) smashing

It never learns chest-to-chest moves or machine work this way. It remembers up to three of these **habits** and jumps
in to help for a few trips now and then. Pick berries in front of one, and it may run off and pick some for you,
dropping them at your feet.

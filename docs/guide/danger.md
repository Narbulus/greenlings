# Danger

Greenlings are fragile (20 HP), and protecting them is part of the game.

- **Animals** are left alone. Greenlings never target boars, deer, chickens, lox and the like, and those
  animals leave greenlings alone unless provoked.
- **Monsters** within 12 m frighten **greylings** (and hurt greydwarfs). They run to you or a **brute**
  and hide behind you, or they panic and scatter.
- Once the threat is gone a scared greenling calms down by itself after a few seconds, or right away if you pat it.
- **Healthy greydwarfs** throw rocks and **brutes** fight, using the vanilla combat AI. **Shamans** cast, and flee once hurt.
- **Shamans heal** hurt greenlings and your animals nearby once things calm down.
- **Wild greylings** hate greenlings.
- When a greenling dies it drops whatever it was carrying, and its tool.

## Doors

Greenlings open ordinary doors when they walk into one, or when that is the only way through, and
close them behind them. Gates, grates and portcullises are too heavy, and so are locked doors.

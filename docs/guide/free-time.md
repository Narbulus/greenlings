# Left to themselves

A greenling without a job, or one that is stuck waiting with empty hands (the chest is full, the hive is empty),
doesn't just stand there. Every couple of seconds it may pick something to do:

## Useful things

- **Tidy up.** Carry a loose item to the chest that already holds that item. Never food, and never a feeding spot.
- **Help a friend.** Lend a hand with one step of a working greenling's job nearby.
- **Habits.** Do a few trips of a chore it picked up by [watching you](teaching.md#learning-by-watching).
- **Repair.** Give one a **hammer** (use it on the greenling) and it goes round fixing pieces below 90% health.
  If something is worn and there's a hammer in a chest nearby, it fetches the hammer itself. It drops the hammer if it dies.
- **Healing and soothing** (shamans): see [Shamans](shamans.md).
- **Rest and decorate.** Pop back to the nest for a rest, or plant a flower or two around it (up to 4 per nest).

Brutes don't tidy.

## Games

- **Tag.** One is *it* and chases the others. A catch swaps roles.
- **Huddle.** A few gather in a ring and natter: chirps, little hops, and turning to face each other.
- **Herding.** Chasing your tamed animals around.
- **Birds.** Running at birds, which take off.
- **Zoomies.** Running about for no reason.

## Mischief

Strays get up to mischief twice as often, and so do greenlings with unmet [needs](upkeep.md). Mischief never
touches your chests:

- **Scatter.** Knocking loose items about.
- **Gifts.** Bringing you a pebble, a flower or a mushroom, like a cat with a bird.
- **Staring.** Just... looking at you.
- **Mimicry.** Copying what you just did: swing, jump, emote.

Other greenlings often stop to watch, or join in, when one gets up to something.

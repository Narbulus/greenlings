# What they can learn

## By role

| Role | Tamed with | Can learn |
|---|---|---|
| **Greyling** | coins | chest to chest; picking up loose items; foraging berries, mushrooms and flowers; collecting honey; keeping fires, hearths and torches fed; keeping a feeding spot stocked; bringing things to you |
| **Greydwarf** | coins | everything a greyling can, plus running machines (smelter, kiln, furnace, windmill, spinning wheel, cooking station, fermenter), gathering stone and flint, and breaking bushes for wood |
| **Brute** | amber, amber pearls | only smashing. Hit a log, rock or stump while it watches, and it smashes every one nearby and leaves the pieces for the little ones to carry. Brutes never fell standing trees. |
| **Shaman** | thistle | everything a greyling can, plus **planting** (only shamans plant). Also heals and soothes strays on its own: see [Shamans](shamans.md). |

They carry one item per trip (a handful of seeds when planting). Greenlings are small. There are no weight limits.

## Job reference

| Job | Show it like this |
|---|---|
| Haul / restock | take an item from one chest, put it in another |
| Tidy up | pick up a loose item off the ground, put it in a chest |
| Smelt | take ore from a chest, add it to the smelter |
| Fuel | take coal or wood from a chest, add it as fuel (smelter, kiln, fire, hearth, torch) |
| Charcoal | take wood from a chest, add it to the kiln |
| Cook | take raw meat from a chest, put it on the cooking station. Then take the cooked meat off and put it in a chest (a two-step job). |
| Brew | take mead base from a chest, put it in the fermenter |
| Honey | take honey from a beehive, put it in a chest |
| Forage | pick a berry bush or mushroom, put what you got in a chest |
| Feeding spot | take food from a chest, drop it on the ground. It keeps a small pile there (2 by default). Your animals will eat from it too. |
| Fetch for me | pick berries while it watches casually, and it brings some to you |
| Smash (brute) | hit a log, stump or rock |
| Plant (shaman) | take seeds from a chest, plant one or two on tilled ground. It fills the patch in rows and replants after harvest. Needs a cultivator. |

## Tools

Some work needs a tool, held in the greenling's hand (one tool at a time):

| Tool | For | Who |
|---|---|---|
| **Hammer** | repairing worn pieces in its free time | anyone but brutes |
| **Cultivator** | planting | shamans |

Hand a greenling its tool by using it on the greenling. Or leave the tool in a chest near the work: a greenling
whose job needs a tool (or that spots something worn) fetches one itself, putting back whatever it was
holding. It looks in the job's own chest first. It drops its tool if it dies.

The [status page](../project/status.md#task-adapters) lists which of these are covered by in-game tests.

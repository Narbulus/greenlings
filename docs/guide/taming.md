# Taming and trust

## Taming

Taming works the same way as taming boars:

- **Greylings and greydwarfs** eat **coins** off the ground. **Brutes** won't stoop to coins: tempt them with **amber** or
  **amber pearls**. **Shamans** prefer **thistle**.
- It eats one item at a time. Each one keeps it fed for a minute, and taming only progresses while
  it is fed **and not alerted**. Stay out of sight, sneak, or build a pen.
- By default taming takes 10 minutes of being fed and calm (about 10 coins). Boars take 30.

Wild greylings hate greenlings and attack them, so tame away from your pets.

## Strays

A freshly tamed creature is a **stray**. It gets a name of its own (Grug, Skorby, Bjolf...), stops being
hostile and wanders around your camp. It gets into mischief but won't learn anything yet.

## Building trust

Trust goes from 0 to 100 and grows when you:

| Do this | Trust | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Feed it | +13 per item | Hand it over (use the food on it), or leave it on the ground. It stays full for a minute. |
| Pat it (++e++) | +5 | Counts once every 30 s. Cuddly ones get double, twice as often. |
| Stay near it | +2 per minute | Within 15 m |

A trusting [shaman](shamans.md) helps too: its company counts like yours, and it comes over to soothe strays now and then.

Food it likes: raspberries, blueberries, cloudberries, mushrooms, honey, carrots, turnips, onions, cooked meat and
Queen's jam.

After a treat or a pat, a stray **tags along** behind you for about 20 seconds, which makes it easy to lead home.

As trust grows it turns greener. Head decorations are part of the authored character assets.
At 100% it becomes a
**Greenling**, gets a personality ([traits](traits.md)), and can be [taught](teaching.md).

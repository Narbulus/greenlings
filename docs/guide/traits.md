# Personalities

Every greenling has a trait, and about a third of them have two. A trait nudges a few numbers so they
feel different, but no trait is strictly better than another. Hover over a greenling to see its traits.

| Trait | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Greedy** | Eager for chores (1.5× as often). Food builds less trust (×0.7). Loves scattering things. |
| **Lazy** | Every action takes 1.7× as long, waits longer between checks, and rarely takes on chores (×0.3). |
| **Clumsy** | Sometimes fumbles what it carries (12% per trip) and drops it. |
| **Curious** | Comes over to watch you 3× as often, and its attention lasts 1.5× as long. |
| **Cuddly** | Pats count double and twice as often. Stares at you a lot, loves a huddle. |
| **Magpie** | Forever bringing you presents. |
| **Zoomy** | Runs everywhere: more zoomies, tag, herding and bird-chasing. |
| **Copycat** | Copies what you do (2.5× as often) and comes to watch you more. |
| **Sleepy** | Calm. Gets into much less mischief (×0.4) and is less curious. |

!!! note "What happened to moods?"
    Earlier versions had a *curiosity* mood (curious / sulky / bored) that you had to manage before teaching.
    After the third playtest that was dropped: a coin always starts a lesson, and looking after
    your greenlings is about [upkeep](upkeep.md) instead.

# Upkeep

Greenlings have two needs: **food** and a **nest**. Neglected greenlings never die or run away, but
for each unmet need they work 1.5× slower and get into more mischief. With both unmet they
sometimes sulk instead of working.

## Food

- A greenling gets hungry 15 minutes after eating.
- Hungry greenlings look for **food lying on the ground** within 20 m. They never take food out of your chests.
- Leave some out, or teach one to keep a **feeding spot** stocked: take food from a chest and drop it on the
  ground in front of it. It keeps a small pile there, and your animals eat from it too.

## Nests

Build a **Greenling Nest** with the hammer, on the furniture tab, for 6 wood and 3 resin.

- A nest within 30 m counts as home. Each nest sleeps **two**. Beds aren't owned: they're shared out again
  every night, so add nests as your crew grows.
- At night greenlings go to sleep in a nest. By day they sometimes pop back for a rest, or plant flowers
  around it.
- A greenling without a bed is **homeless**, which counts as an unmet need. Who misses out is decided by a
  fixed pecking order, so it's the same on every player's screen.

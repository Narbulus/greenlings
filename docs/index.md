# Greenlings

*Tame greylings (and greydwarfs, and brutes), earn their trust, and teach them to do your chores.*

Greenlings is a mod for **Valheim** (BepInEx 5). It is a from-scratch revival inspired by
RagnarsRokare's *SlaveGreylings*. Instead of enslaving them, you befriend them. You show a
greenling a chore once, and it keeps doing it, one clumsy armful at a time.

## At a glance

- **Two-stage taming.** Coins (or amber for brutes) tame a wild one into a *stray*. Food, pats
  and company build its trust until it becomes a **Greenling**.
- **Teaching by demonstration.** Give it a coin, then take something from one place and put it in another.
  It learns the job, and the job stretches to cover every beehive, torch or kiln of that kind nearby.
- **Roles.** Greylings do light chores. Greydwarfs also run machines. Brutes smash logs and rocks.
- **A life of their own.** They have personalities, tidy up, help each other, play tag, huddle for a
  natter, bring you presents, and need food and a nest.
